# One-Pager — for the owner

## Your website has 91 products and no prices

Every page ends with "Cek Harga dan Ketersediaan". That means your WhatsApp
admin **is** your checkout. Everything depends on two people being awake,
reading, and asking the right follow-up questions.

That works at today's volume. It does not scale, and it leaks in three specific
places.

## Where it leaks

**1. Every inquiry starts from zero.**
The buyer sends "mau tanya flow meter solar buat pertamina 2 nozzle". Someone
has to work out the part, the quantity, the city, and the urgency before a
price can be given. Same reconstruction, hundreds of times.

**2. Overnight inquiries wait until morning.**
A plant engineer asking at 22:00 is comparing three suppliers. Whoever answers
first usually wins. You answer second.

**3. A stockout ends the conversation.**
When a part is unavailable, the thread stalls. Your catalog carries many
interchangeable brands — but nobody has time to find the equivalent in the
middle of a busy day.

## What we build

A WhatsApp assistant that turns a messy inquiry into a **price-ready RFQ** for
your admin.

It does three things:

**Understands the catalog.** Answers product and spec questions from your 91
products, and confirms fitment — size, pressure, flow, fluid, flange — before
anything is promised.

**Builds the RFQ.** Captures item, quantity, delivery city, urgency, and buyer
type into one record, then hands it to the admin with everything needed to
price in 30 seconds.

**Handles stockouts.** Says honestly whether a part is available, indent, or
needs checking, and offers a credible equivalent instead of letting the thread
die.

## What it deliberately does not do

- It does not quote prices. Price stays with your admin. Always.
- It does not state stock it cannot verify.
- It does not negotiate.
- It does not replace your team. It makes them faster.

These are design choices, not limitations. They are why it is safe to put in
front of your buyers.

## What changes

| Today | After |
|---|---|
| Inquiry waits hours for a reply | Answered in seconds, 24/7 |
| Admin reconstructs each inquiry | Admin receives a complete RFQ |
| Stockout ends the thread | Equivalent offered in the same thread |
| No record of what was asked | Every inquiry logged, weekly demand report |
| Growth requires hiring | Growth does not |

## How we start

**Week 1 —** Watch how you actually work. Record real conversations, confirm
where price and stock live.

**Weeks 2–3 —** Build the catalog and fitment layer. Nothing goes live until it
can answer your products correctly.

**Weeks 4–5 —** RFQ capture and admin handoff, on a test number.

**Week 6 —** Availability and substitution. Then shadow mode: the assistant
drafts, your team approves every message.

Live only after your admins say it is faster than doing it themselves.

## What we need from you

- Two days of shadowing your admins
- Datasheets for products whose specs are thin on the site
- One technical contact to verify equivalences between brands
- Your answer on where stock actually lives today

## The honest caveat

The catalog on your site is uneven. Some products have full specs, some have two
lines, some have copy-pasted text about aviation refuelling in Illinois. We will
tell you exactly which products the assistant can answer confidently and which
ones it will hand to a human.

We would rather publish a coverage number than pretend it is 100%.
